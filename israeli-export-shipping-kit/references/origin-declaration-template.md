# Invoice Origin Declaration Templates

Use these exact wordings. Print on the commercial invoice, packing list, or proforma. Sign manually.

## EU-Israel (and UK-Israel) - Invoice Declaration

Use for shipments up to 6000 euros (any exporter) or for any value if approved-exporter.

> The exporter of the products covered by this document (customs authorisation No ...) declares that, except where otherwise clearly indicated, these products are of ISRAEL preferential origin.
>
> [Name of the city, village or industrial zone AND postal code where the origin-conferring production took place] (mandatory on the EU route under the EU-Israel Technical Arrangement)
>
> [Place and date]
>
> [Signature of the exporter; name of the signatory written in clear]

This is the English text of the origin declaration annexed to the revised (2023) PEM rules that the EU route now uses (Decision No 1/2023 of the PEM Joint Committee); the 2012 text used on the EFTA route has the same structure. Replace the authorisation number with your approved-exporter number, or omit "(customs authorisation No ...)" if you are not approved (permitted only up to 6000 euros of originating goods).

## US-Israel FTA - Exporter origin statement (Israel to US)

For Israel-to-US shipments CBP requires no certificate and no exporter declaration: it eliminated the "green" Form A for imports from Israel on 20 May 1994 and instead may request "a signed affidavit upon request by CBP from the importer acknowledging that the good meets the origination and the shipping requirements of the FTA". CBP adds that the 2017 amendment "represents no change in the ILFTA requirements for importations into the United States". The "U.S. Origin Invoice Declaration" and its 10 January 2018 start date belong to the opposite direction, US exporters shipping to Israel. To qualify, the cost or value of materials produced in Israel plus the direct costs of processing in Israel must be at least 35 percent of the appraised value of the article (up to 15 percent of the appraised value may come from US materials).

So the statement below is VOLUNTARY: it gives your US buyer a signed basis for the affidavit. The wording below is a suggested form, not a prescribed text.

> I, the undersigned, hereby declare that unless otherwise indicated, the goods covered by this document fully comply with the rules of origin and the other provisions of the Agreement on the Establishment of a Free Trade Area between the Government of Israel and the Government of the United States of America.
>
> I hereby undertake to provide such supporting evidence regarding the origin status of the goods as may be required by the customs authorities of the importing country.
>
> [Israeli exporter name, address]
>
> [Invoice number and date]
>
> [Manually signed]

Sources: https://www.cbp.gov/trade/free-trade-agreements/israel/certificate-origin-requirements (Israel to US) and https://www.trade.gov/us-israel-free-trade-agreement (35 percent rule; the declaration for US exporters).

## Canada-Israel FTA (CIFTA)

This skill is for ISRAELI exporters shipping TO Canada. Under the modernized CIFTA (in force September 1, 2019) YOU, the Israeli exporter, complete and sign Form B239 (Canada-Israel Free Trade Agreement Certificate of Origin) and send it with the shipment; it is not the Canadian buyer's paperwork. The certificate is valid 4 years from signature.

A blank invoice declaration does NOT substitute for Form B239 under CIFTA. Always issue the form, EXCEPT that for commercial goods imported on or after July 1, 2020 whose value for duty does not exceed CAD 3,300, "a proof of origin is not required" at all. Where the goods underwent minor processing in a non-Party on the way, Form E669 (Declaration of Minor Processing in the territory of a Non-Party) is required in addition to the B239.

Source: https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/publications/dm-md/d11/d11-4-2-eng.html.

## EFTA-Israel

Use the EUR.1 movement certificate or an invoice declaration with the same wording as the EU-Israel declaration (substitute "EFTA" / destination country name in the appropriate box). EFTA-Israel is still on the 2012 PEM rules, so the proof is valid 4 months, not the 10 months of the EU route.

## Mercosur-Israel

Use a Mercosur-Israel movement certificate. Invoice-declaration wording is not standard for this FTA; request the certificate via Israeli Customs.

## Signature requirements

- Invoice declarations (EU, UK, EFTA) and the voluntary US exporter statement: sign by hand.
- CIFTA Form B239: signed by the exporter.
- EUR.1 certificates are a different case: Israeli Customs endorses them, and since 1 April 2024 it can issue them electronically (see `pem-2026-rules.md`).
- All: print the signatory's name and position in clear below the signature.

## Record keeping

Keep origin calculations, supplier declarations and copies of each declaration for the retention period of the agreement concerned (confirm it with your broker for each route). Israeli Customs may verify any origin claim with the foreign customs authority.
